Vowgate

Privacy

Effective 3 September 2026 · Buildathon demonstration

Vowgate is a public technical demonstration. It has no user accounts, advertising trackers, or live-commerce capability.

Information you provide

The transaction lab starts with a synthetic purchase instruction. If intent interpretation is configured, that text may be sent to Google Gemini to produce structured constraints. Do not enter personal, confidential, or real payment information.

Test payments

Vowgate opens Razorpay Checkout in Test Mode only. Razorpay processes the test checkout interaction under its own privacy terms. The application verifies returned test payment identifiers and signatures; it does not accept live credentials or move real money.

Operational data

Our hosting provider may retain ordinary request, error, security, and performance logs. Vowgate does not intentionally build user profiles, sell data, or use behavioral advertising.

Open source

The implementation and its data flows are available in the public repository. Never submit secrets through GitHub issues.

Contact

For a privacy question, contact the project maintainer through the repository. This notice will be updated if the demonstration begins collecting additional data.
